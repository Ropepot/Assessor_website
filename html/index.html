About the Office

The Municipal Assessor's Office of Oas is responsible for the appraisal and assessment of real properties within the municipality for taxation purposes. It ensures that real property records are correct and updated, tax declarations are correctly issued, and proper assessment procedures are implemented in accordance with existing regulations.

New Declaration on Land, Building, & Machinery Process

  1. Document Submission: Submit complete requirements to the receiving officer for preliminary evaluation.
  2. Verification & Appraisal: Office staff verify existing property records and conduct field inspection or appraisal when necessary.
  3. Approval: The Tax Declaration is reviewed and approved by the Municipal Assessor and Provincial Assessor, as applicable.
  4. Issuance: The updated Tax Declaration is released to the property owner or authorized representative.

Issuance of Certifications

The Municipal Assessor's Office issues the following certifications:

  • Certificate of No Property
  • Certificate of Landholdings
  • Certificate of Improvements
  • Certificate of Without Improvements

Issuance Fee: ₱80.00 per certification, payable at the Treasurer's Office.

Transfer of Real Property

Required documentation for processing:

  • Deed of Conveyance (Deed of Absolute Sale, Donation, Extrajudicial Settlement, etc.)
  • Special Power of Attorney (SPA), if represented by an authorized representative
  • Transfer Tax Receipt
  • Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR) from the BIR
  • Real Property Tax Clearance (current year)
  • Latest Tax Declaration copy
  • Valid identification cards of involved parties
  • Approved Survey Plan (subdivision, consolidation, etc.)
  • DAR Clearance, if applicable to agricultural property
  • Other supporting documents, if required
